The Open Hearth Kitchen

Culinary Journey

More Than Just a Meal

Founded with a belief that dining should be an unhurried celebration of flavor and togetherness, Bella Kitchen began as a neighborhood gathering spot in Lagos. We source our produce every morning from trusted local markets and regional farmers, preparing every sauce, stock, and marinade from scratch.

Whether you are joining us for a celebratory weekend feast or a quiet weeknight supper, we welcome you with warm Nigerian hospitality and culinary craftsmanship you can taste in every bite.

Chef Amara Okafor

Founding Chef & Creative Lead
